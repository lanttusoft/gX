# Code of Conduct

gX is a small beta project. Participation should be respectful, specific, and
focused on making the engine and its documentation better.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code and behavior, not people.
- Assume good faith, but be precise when reporting a problem.
- Respect privacy: do not publish another person's private information,
  credentials, logs, or assets.
- Keep criticism actionable and scope discussions to the repository.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, and sustained disruption are not welcome. Do not use project
spaces to distribute malware, private game material, or unlicensed assets.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-related
communication. The maintainer may edit, lock, or remove contributions and may
restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its participants.

Report a concern privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile.
For platform-level abuse, use GitHub's report-abuse flow. Do not open a public
issue containing private or sensitive details. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as the available channels allow.
