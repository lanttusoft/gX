# Support

gX is a public beta provided as source, without a support SLA.

- **Documentation:** [`docs/README.md`](docs/README.md) is the index. Start
  with the build, renderer, tooling, and API pages relevant to the subsystem.
- **Bug reports:** use the repository's bug-report template. Include the exact
  version or commit, operating system, display server, GPU/driver, build mode,
  reproduction steps, and relevant logs.
- **Feature requests:** use the feature-request template and describe the
  engine problem first, then the proposed behavior.
- **Security issues:** do not open a public issue. Follow `SECURITY.md` and use
  the private vulnerability-reporting channel.
- **Private material:** do not attach gameX assets, story content, private
  scenes, credentials, or personal data to a public report.

The beta is tested primarily on Linux/X11. Reports from other platforms are
useful, but support may be limited by available hardware and drivers.
